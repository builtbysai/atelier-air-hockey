How to play

Mouse or trackpad: move the pointer and the mallet follows directly. No click needed. Fast pointer movement creates a harder strike.

Keyboard or gamepad: use WASD, arrow keys, or the left stick. Small taps make precise corrections; holding a direction builds speed.

Drag your mallet directly across your half of the table. Flick quickly to smash the puck; ease it to deaden and control.

Your mallet can't cross the center line. First to 7 goals wins the table.

Rally: each return by the other side adds one. Repeated touches by the same mallet count once.

Two players: each takes an end. On touch screens, use two touches. On desktop, use WASD + arrow keys or two gamepads.

Shortcuts: P pause · M mute all · Esc pause/resume. Gamepad Menu/Start also pauses.
