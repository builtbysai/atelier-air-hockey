How to play

Move your mallet with mouse, WASD, arrow keys, or a gamepad. Flick it to smash the puck; ease it to deaden and control.

Drag your mallet directly across your half of the table. Flick quickly to smash the puck; ease it to deaden and control.

Your mallet can't cross the center line. First to 7 goals wins the table.

Two players: each takes an end. On touch screens, use two touches. On desktop, use WASD + arrow keys or two gamepads.

Keys: P pause · M mute all · Esc pause/resume.
