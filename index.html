How to play

Move your mallet with mouse or finger — it tracks you one-to-one. Flick it to smash the puck; ease it to deaden and control.

Your mallet can't cross the center line. First to 7 goals wins the table.

Two players: each takes an end — mouse and touch work at once, or two fingers on mobile.

Effects scales the spectacle — particles, flashes, confetti, room glow — without touching the physics. Minimal also caps screen shake at Subtle.

Keys: P pause · M mute · Esc menu.
